TASK: EMAIL INTENT PARSING

Analyze this email received by a job seeker and extract actionable information.

Intents:
- acknowledgment: automated "we received your application"
- rejection: the company is not moving forward
- interview_invite: invitation to schedule or confirmation of an interview / phone screen
- assessment: coding challenge, take-home, online test
- offer: job offer
- follow_up: recruiter checking in / asking for availability without a concrete interview
- info_request: the company asks for documents or information
- generic: job-related but none of the above (newsletters, job alerts)

status_update must be one of: acknowledged, rejected, screening, interview, assessment,
final_round, offer, or "none" when the application status should not change.
Use "screening" for recruiter/phone screens and "interview" for technical/onsite rounds.

(candidate timezone: {{timezone}}) - resolve relative dates like "next Tuesday" against it.

(empty string for rejections that need no reply and for automated acknowledgments).
